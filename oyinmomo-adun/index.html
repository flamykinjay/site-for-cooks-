01 — Sourcing

Sweetness starts at the market, not the stove.

Ripe tatashe peppers, sun-red tomatoes, fresh tubers and long-grain rice — chosen by hand from the same stalls, every single week.

Sourced fresh, daily

Fresh, before flame.

02 — Seasoning

Pepper, onion, heat — where the flavour is born.

Our blended pepper base and house yaji spice mix are made from scratch and left to rest before they ever meet a flame.

House-blended spice

03 — Cooking

Then we let the pot do its quiet work.

Rice steams low and slow for that bottom-pot crust everyone quietly hopes for; skewers turn over open coals until the edges catch. No shortcuts, no rushing it.

Cooked to order, not reheated

04 — Plating

Every tray gets its own final check.

We taste, adjust, and finish each dish the way we'd want it handed to us — hot, generous, and just a little bit extra.

Plated with intention

05 — Serving

Arrive hungry. Leave talking about it.

From a twenty-guest naming ceremony to a four-hundred-guest owambe, we bring the same kitchen discipline to every size of room.
